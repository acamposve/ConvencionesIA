# Feature Specification

**Feature:** Text Normalization

**Status:** Draft

**Version:** 1.0.0

---

# Goal

Transform the extracted raw text into a normalized, structured, and machine-friendly representation while preserving its original semantic meaning.

The normalized text becomes the canonical input for downstream AI-powered features such as Clause Detection, Clause Categorization, Document Classification, and Document Summarization.

---

# Business Value

Documents extracted from different formats and OCR engines often contain inconsistent formatting, broken lines, duplicated whitespace, encoding issues, and extraction artifacts.

Normalizing the extracted text improves:

- AI accuracy
- Clause detection quality
- Consistency across document formats
- Repeatability of document processing
- Semantic search quality
- Embedding generation quality

---

# Scope

This feature is responsible only for normalizing extracted text.

It does not:

- Detect clauses
- Categorize clauses
- Modify document meaning
- Translate text
- Correct grammar
- Summarize content
- Generate embeddings
- Perform AI inference

---

# Functional Requirements

## FR-001

The service shall normalize the RawText generated by the Text Extraction feature.

---

## FR-002

The service shall preserve the semantic meaning of the original text.

---

## FR-003

The service shall preserve the original reading order.

---

## FR-004

The service shall normalize line endings.

---

## FR-005

The service shall normalize whitespace.

Examples include:

- consecutive spaces
- tabs
- trailing spaces
- multiple blank lines

---

## FR-006

The service shall normalize Unicode characters.

---

## FR-007

The service shall normalize quotation marks.

---

## FR-008

The service shall normalize dash and hyphen characters.

---

## FR-009

The service shall remove non-printable characters.

---

## FR-010

The service shall preserve paragraph boundaries whenever possible.

---

## FR-011

The service shall preserve numbering structures including:

- Clause numbers
- Section numbers
- Lists
- Bullet points

---

## FR-012

The service shall produce a NormalizedText representation.

---

## FR-013

The original RawText shall remain unchanged.

---

## FR-014

If normalization fails, processing shall stop and the document shall transition to Failed status.

---

# Business Rules

## BR-001

Normalization shall never modify the legal meaning of the document.

---

## BR-002

Normalization shall never invent content.

---

## BR-003

Normalization shall never remove meaningful text.

---

## BR-004

Normalization shall only perform deterministic transformations.

---

## BR-005

The same RawText shall always produce the same NormalizedText.

---

## BR-006

RawText is immutable.

---

## BR-007

NormalizedText becomes the canonical textual representation for downstream processing.

# Events

## TextNormalized

Published after successful normalization.

Example payload

- DocumentId
- TenantId
- OriginalLength
- NormalizedLength
- NormalizedAt
- CorrelationId

---

## TextNormalizationFailed

Published when normalization cannot be completed.

Example payload

- DocumentId
- TenantId
- FailureReason
- CorrelationId

# Processing Flow

Document

Status = TextExtracted

↓

Load RawText

↓

Normalize Text

↓

Generate NormalizedText

↓

Store NormalizedText

↓

Publish TextNormalized

↓

Continue to Clause Detection

Failure path

↓

Normalization Error

↓

Publish TextNormalizationFailed

↓

Document.Status = Failed

---

# Non-Functional Requirements

## Performance

Normalization should complete within acceptable operational limits for documents up to 50 pages.

---

## Reliability

Normalization shall be deterministic.

---

## Scalability

The feature shall support asynchronous execution.

---

## Security

Normalization shall not expose document contents through logs.

---

## Observability

The following information shall be logged:

- DocumentId
- TenantId
- ProcessingTime
- OriginalLength
- NormalizedLength
- CorrelationId

---

# Error Conditions

The feature shall detect and report:

- Missing RawText
- Empty RawText
- Invalid Unicode sequences
- Internal normalization failures
- Storage failures

Errors shall not expose implementation details.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

## AC-001

Given extracted text containing multiple consecutive spaces

When normalization executes

Then consecutive spaces shall be reduced to a single space where appropriate.

---

## AC-002

Given extracted text containing inconsistent line endings

When normalization executes

Then line endings shall be normalized.

---

## AC-003

Given extracted text containing tabs

When normalization executes

Then tabs shall be normalized consistently.

---

## AC-004

Given extracted text containing non-printable characters

When normalization executes

Then non-printable characters shall be removed.

---

## AC-005

Given extracted text containing Unicode variations

When normalization executes

Then Unicode characters shall be normalized consistently.

---

## AC-006

Given extracted text containing numbered clauses

When normalization executes

Then clause numbering shall remain unchanged.

---

## AC-007

Given valid RawText

When normalization completes

Then NormalizedText shall be stored in the Document Aggregate.

---

## AC-008

Given missing RawText

When normalization executes

Then processing shall fail.

---

## AC-009

Given a normalization failure

When processing stops

Then TextNormalizationFailed shall be published.

---

- Document classification
- Document summarization
- Translation
- Grammar correction
- Spelling correction
